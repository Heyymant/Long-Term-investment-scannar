Keep ranks between top-N and 2× top-N so noise around the cut does not churn. No percent stop-loss. Tax-defer near 365 days.

Exit past 2× top-N, or if profitability z falls below zero after leaving top-N. Overlay: park the book if the 200-day average is down more than 2%.

Why these rules (papers, tax, holding days)
  • Three books: IIMA profitability (core), value×quality (cheap-and-good), skip-month momentum (satellite).
  • Rank buffer follows Jegadeesh–Titman skip-month and IIMA Fama–French–Momentum.
  • STCG 20% vs LTCG 12.5% — hold through tax year-end when a name is near 365 days.
  • Core compounders hold ~11–12 months; momentum satellite ~4–5 months (holding days ≈ 252 / turnover).
  • Do not stack Research-rail chips. Those recipes failed or were untested on this window.
